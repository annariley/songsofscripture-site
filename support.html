Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Spotify account?

A free or premium Spotify account is needed for music features (search, save, and play songs). You can browse Scripture without an account using Guest mode.

How do I save a song to my Spotify library?

Tap the heart icon on any song card. You'll need to be signed in with your Spotify account. The song will appear in your Liked Songs on Spotify.

How do I disconnect my Spotify account?

Go to your Profile tab and tap "Sign Out." You can also revoke access from your Spotify account settings at spotify.com/account/apps.

What Bible translation is used?

We use the World English Bible (WEB), a modern public domain translation that's readable and freely distributable.

Can I suggest a song to add?

Yes! Use the feedback form above with the song name, artist, and which passage it relates to. We review all suggestions.

How do I delete my data?

Submit a request through the feedback form or open an issue. We'll delete all data associated with your account within 48 hours.

Why can't I hear full songs?

We show 30-second previews when available. For full playback, tap "Open in Spotify" to listen in the Spotify app.
