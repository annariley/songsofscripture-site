FAQ

Do I need a Spotify account?

A free or premium Spotify account is required to use music features (search, save, and play songs). You can browse Scripture without an account using Guest mode.

How do I disconnect my Spotify account?

Go to your Profile tab and tap "Sign Out." You can also revoke access from your Spotify account settings at spotify.com.

How do I delete my data?

Contact us at support@songsofscripture.com and we will delete all data associated with your account.
