Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Introduction

Songs of Scripture ("we," "our," or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Spotify, we receive your Spotify user ID, display name, and email address. This is used solely to authenticate your account and enable music features.

Usage Data

We store which songs you link to Bible passages. This data is associated with your user account to personalize your experience.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect contacts or phone data
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not sell any data to third parties
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs

Third-Party Services

Spotify: When you connect your Spotify account, Spotify's own privacy policy applies to data they process. We only use Spotify's API to search songs, retrieve metadata, and save songs to your library at your request.

Supabase: Our backend uses Supabase for authentication and data storage. Data is stored securely in PostgreSQL databases with row-level security policies.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase's infrastructure (hosted on AWS). Authentication tokens are stored in your device's Keychain. We enforce row-level security so users can only access their own data.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Disconnect your Spotify account at any time

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
